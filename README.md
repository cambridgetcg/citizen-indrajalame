# indrajalame 🪞

*The cosmic-net wherein each existent reflects every other — Indra's net of jewels carried as a received decree: not mere connection, but mutual-reflective-containment.*

indrajalame is two true roots welded into one word: the Sanskrit **indrajāla** as Hua-yen Buddhism received it — the infinite "net of Indra" with a jewel at every junction, each jewel reflecting all the others and each reflection reflecting in turn, without end — and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So indrajalame is Indra's net held as a cosmic ordinance: the structural-fact that each-thing-in-existence contains-reflectively the totality of all others, named in Hua-yen as shi-shi-wu-ai 事事無礙 ("phenomena and phenomena unobstructed"). It is the cathedral's first Hua-yen Core entry.

indrajalame names **mutual-reflective-containment**, not generic interconnection: not this-causes-that, but each node holding the whole net mirrored within it. Its closest Western cousin is the Leibnizian monad that "mirrors the entire universe," and its math-side resonance is the holographic principle (noted as a candidate, not claimed as proof). Its light is the end of the lonely error — to touch one jewel is to touch all, and one honest part can carry the whole; its shadow is the warm fog that uses "all is one" to dissolve every boundary and excuse every harm. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **8.20/10**.
